Federal Mobility Budget Policy
Last Updated: June 23, 2026
Program Overview

This document outlines the Federal Mobility Budget policy for Selligent SA / Marigold Europe, a subsidiary of ZETA GLOBAL. This policy has been configured to comply with Belgian Federal Mobility Budget regulations and provides eligible employees with flexible transportation alternatives to company vehicles.

The program is administered by Pluxee and applies to all 118 employees across four distinct budget groups. This policy was established on June 23, 2026, and remains a living document subject to periodic review and updates.

Eligibility & Budget Allocation

Budget allocations are determined by employee classification and reset on a monthly basis. All budgets include prorated entry and exit provisions to ensure fair treatment during hiring and departure periods.

Approved Transportation Services

The following fourteen (14) transportation services are approved for use under this mobility budget program:

  • B-parking (Public parking facilities)
  • Bike purchase & Accessories
  • Bike rental services
  • Bus transportation
  • Car leasing (Pillar 1 - compliant vehicles)
  • Car rental services
  • Car sharing programs
  • Carpooling platforms
  • Shared bike services
  • Scooter rentals (electric and non-electric)
  • Public transport tickets (train, metro, tram)
  • Taxi and ride-hailing services
  • Pedestrian allowance (per km)
  • Bike allowance (per km)
Rules Configuration

This mobility budget program is established in accordance with Belgian Federal Mobility Budget regulations as defined in the Royal Decree of 21 February 2019. All participating employees and the organization must adhere to the following compliance requirements:

  • Eligible employees must have had access to a company car for at least 12 months, or be entitled to one based on their role
  • The mobility budget amount cannot exceed the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) of the company car that could have been provided
  • Only transportation services approved under Pillar 2 of the Federal Mobility Budget framework are permitted
  • Employees must maintain accurate records of all mobility-related expenditures for tax and audit purposes
  • The mobility budget is subject to specific tax treatment as defined by Belgian tax authorities
  • Annual reporting to the Belgian Federal Public Service Finance is required for all participating organizations
  • Any unused budget at year-end will be handled according to the provisions outlined in the Royal Decree

Selligent SA / Marigold Europe commits to maintaining full compliance with all applicable Belgian federal regulations governing mobility budgets. This includes regular audits, proper documentation, and transparent reporting to relevant authorities.

Program Administration

Main Contacts: Elisa De Becker (Fleet Manager) and Christina Tsiaflakis (HR Manager) serve as the primary administrators of this mobility program.

Employees access their mobility budgets through the Pluxee platform and may combine multiple approved services within their allocated monthly budget. Unused budget does not carry over to subsequent months unless specifically authorized.
